## 1. Student Health, Wellness & Psychological Services

### 1.1 Counseling and Psychological Services (CAPS)
- **Eligibility & Access:** All currently registered students paying student wellness fees are eligible for confidential individual counseling, group therapy, and wellness workshops.
- **Appointment Scheduling:** Non-emergency initial consultations can be scheduled through the student health portal or by calling the wellness intake line (Monday – Friday, 08:30 to 17:00).
- **Crisis & After-Hours Support:** 
  - A dedicated 24/7 crisis support counselor is accessible after hours and on weekends via the main campus crisis line.
  - In situations of imminent emergency, students should contact Campus Safety & Police or dial local emergency services (911).
- **Session Limits:** Students receive up to 10 complimentary individual therapy sessions per academic year. Referrals to specialized community providers are coordinated for long-term therapeutic care.

### 1.2 Student Health Clinic & Medical Care
- **Services Covered:** Basic acute illness visits, minor laceration management, routine physical examinations, sexual health consultations, immunizations, and allergy injections.
- **Consultation Fees:** Standard provider appointments are 100% covered by mandatory student fees. Specialized laboratory tests, prescription medications, and durable medical equipment (splints, crutches) carry nominal discounted charges billed to student health insurance or campus accounts.
- **Required Immunizations:** All incoming students must submit verified documentation for Measles, Mumps, Rubella (MMR), Meningococcal ACWY, Tdap, and Hepatitis B prior to class registration.

## 2. Housing, Residential Life & Campus Dining

### 2.1 On-Campus Residence Halls
- **Housing Application Window:** First-year housing applications open on February 1st with priority placement guaranteed for applications received by May 1st.
- **Room Selection & Roommates:** Returning students participate in online room lottery selection during the spring term. Mutual roommate requests must be confirmed by both parties before the matching deadline.
- **Move-In / Move-Out Dates:**
  - Fall Semester Move-In: The Thursday preceding the first instructional day.
  - Spring Semester Move-In: The Sunday preceding instruction.
  - Residence halls close 24 hours after the conclusion of final examinations each semester.

### 2.2 Dining Services & Meal Plans
- **Meal Plan Options:** 
  - *Unlimited Plan:* Unlimited visits to all residential dining commons plus $250 Dining Dollars per term.
  - *Block 150 / Block 200:* Fixed number of meal swipes per semester with rollover dining dollars.
  - *Commuter Flex Plan:* Decrementing dining dollar balance with a 10% discount at all campus retail eateries.
- **Dietary Accommodations:** Halal, Kosher, vegan, gluten-free, and allergen-safe preparation stations are available at all main dining commons. Students with severe food allergies may request consultations with the Campus Registered Dietitian.

## 3. Transportation, Parking & Campus Transit

### 3.1 Campus Shuttle Network
- **Fixed Routes:** The Campus Transit System operates three complimentary shuttle lines (Blue Line, Gold Line, Night Owl Route) serving residence halls, academic quads, commuter parking structures, and adjacent transit stations.
- **Operating Hours:** Shuttles run every 10–15 minutes from 06:30 to 22:30 on weekdays. The Night Owl safety shuttle runs on demand from 22:30 to 03:00 daily.
- **Real-Time GPS Tracking:** Shuttle locations and estimated arrival times are accessible in real time via the university mobile app.

### 3.2 Commuter & Resident Parking Permits
- **Permit Types:**
  - *Zone C (Commuter):* Grants parking access to perimeter commuter lots from 07:00 to 23:00.
  - *Zone R (Resident):* Dedicated residential garage parking for students residing in campus housing.
  - *Evening / Weekend Pass:* Valid in all general faculty and commuter lots after 16:30 and on weekends.
- **Enforcement:** Parking regulations are strictly enforced year-round. Vehicles parked without an active virtual permit or in reserved ADA spaces without state placards are subject to immediate citation.

## 4. Student Accessibility & Disability Services

### 4.1 Academic & Housing Accommodations
- The Office of Student Accessibility Services ensures equal access for learners with permanent or temporary documented disabilities (physical, psychological, sensory, neurodivergent).
- **Common Accommodations:** 
  - Extended testing time (1.5x or 2.0x standard duration) in low-distraction environments.
  - Assistive audio recording and peer note-taking assistance.
  - Accessible digital courseware, screen-reader compatible PDFs, and closed-captioned lectures.
  - Single-occupancy housing accommodations with private bathrooms for documented medical needs.

### 4.2 Accommodation Intake Process
- Students must submit comprehensive diagnostic documentation from a licensed healthcare provider at least 4 weeks prior to the start of the term.
- Accommodations are non-retroactive; students must deliver verified *Faculty Notification Letters* to course instructors during the first two weeks of class.

## 5. Student Organizations, Leadership & Campus Recreation

### 5.1 Recognized Student Organizations (RSOs)
- Over 250 active student groups, including academic honor societies, cultural organizations, pre-professional clubs, Greek life, and special interest hobbies.
- **Starting a New Club:** Requires a minimum of 5 enrolled student members, a faculty/staff advisor, and an approved constitution submitted through the Office of Student Involvement.

### 5.2 Campus Recreation Center
- **Facilities:** Multi-court gymnasium, 25-meter lap swimming pool, indoor rock climbing wall, Olympic lifting platforms, cardio mezzanine, and indoor running track.
- **Access:** Included in tuition and fee schedule; activated automatically upon presenting a valid student ID card.
- **Intramural Sports:** Seasonal recreational leagues (soccer, basketball, volleyball, flag football, badminton) open to all skill levels with no varsity eligibility required.
