# Institutional Academic Policies & Degree Regulations

> **Scope:** General Academic Regulations for Undergraduate & Graduate Degree Programs  
> **Target Audience:** Enrolled Students, Academic Advisors, and Department Chairs  
> **Botpress Knowledge Base Category:** `academic-advising`

---

## 1. Credit Hour System & Course Registration

### 1.1 Course Load Classifications
- **Full-Time Undergraduate:** A student enrolled in a minimum of 12 credit hours and a maximum of 18 credit hours per standard academic semester (Fall/Spring).
- **Part-Time Undergraduate:** Any student enrolled in fewer than 12 credit hours per semester.
- **Credit Overload Policy:** Students possessing a cumulative grade point average (CGPA) of 3.50 or higher may petition to register for up to 21 credit hours with written authorization from their Academic Dean.
- **Summer / Winter Sessions:** Students may enroll in a maximum of 7 credit hours per condensed term.

### 1.2 Registration & Course Schedule Adjustments
- **Priority Registration:** Registration windows open in phased cohorts determined by earned credit hours (Seniors -> Juniors -> Sophomores -> First-Year Students).
- **Add / Drop Period:** Students may freely adjust course schedules without financial or academic record implications during the first 10 instructional days of the term.
- **Course Withdrawal ("W"):** 
  - Initiated after the census date (Day 10) through the end of the 10th instructional week.
  - A notation of "W" appears on the permanent transcript.
  - The "W" carries no grade points and has zero impact on GPA, but counts toward total attempted hours for financial aid completion rate metrics.
  - Maximum permitted course withdrawals over an undergraduate degree: 5 courses.

## 2. Prerequisite & Corequisite Requirements

### 2.1 Enforcement & Automated Screening
- Course prerequisites are strictly enforced at the point of electronic registration.
- A grade of **C- (1.70)** or higher is typically required in all prerequisite coursework unless a department specifies a minimum of **C (2.00)** for foundational major courses.

### 2.2 Prerequisite Waivers
- In cases where a transfer student or exceptional learner possesses verified equivalent competencies, a *Prerequisite Waiver Form* must be endorsed by both the course instructor and the respective Department Head prior to the registration deadline.

## 3. Grading Standard & Grade Point Average (GPA)

### 3.1 Official Grading Scale
The institution operates on a standard four-point (4.00) quality point system:

| Letter Grade | Grade Points | Percentage Equivalent | Description |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **A** | 4.00 | 93% – 100% | Exceptional Mastery |
| **A-** | 3.70 | 90% – 92% | Superior Performance |
| **B+** | 3.30 | 87% – 89% | Very Good Competency |
| **B** | 3.00 | 83% – 86% | Solid Competency |
| **B-** | 2.70 | 80% – 82% | Above Average Competency |
| **C+** | 2.30 | 77% – 79% | Average Competency |
| **C** | 2.00 | 73% – 76% | Satisfactory Completion (Minimum for Major Requirements) |
| **C-** | 1.70 | 70% – 72% | Passing, Below Average |
| **D+** | 1.30 | 67% – 69% | Marginal Passing |
| **D** | 1.00 | 60% – 66% | Minimum Passing (Non-Major Electives Only) |
| **F** | 0.00 | Below 60% | Failure to Meet Learning Objectives |
| **I** | N/A | Incomplete | Temporary non-punitive mark granted for medical/documented hardship |
| **P / NP** | N/A | Pass / No Pass | Selected general education electives only |

### 3.2 Incomplete Grade ("I") Policy
- An Incomplete may only be granted when a student has completed at least 70% of course coursework with a passing grade, but encounters unforeseen documented extenuating circumstances (e.g., serious medical emergency, bereavement).
- The remaining coursework must be finalized within the first 6 weeks of the subsequent regular academic semester, after which an unfulfilled "I" automatically converts to an "F".

## 4. Academic Standing & Retention Regulations

### 4.1 Good Academic Standing
- An undergraduate student is maintained in Good Academic Standing whenever their cumulative grade point average (CGPA) remains at or above **2.00**.

### 4.2 Academic Warning & Probation
- **Academic Warning:** Issued if a student's term GPA falls below 2.00 in any single semester while their cumulative GPA remains at or above 2.00.
- **Academic Probation:** Imposed when a student's cumulative GPA drops below 2.00.
  - Students on Academic Probation may register for a maximum of 13 credit hours.
  - Students must collaborate with the Academic Success Center to construct a personalized *Academic Recovery Agreement*.
  - Failure to raise the cumulative GPA to 2.00 or attain a semester GPA of 2.25 across two consecutive probationary terms results in academic suspension.

### 4.3 Dean's Honor List
- Awarded at the conclusion of each regular semester to full-time undergraduate students who complete at least 12 graded credit hours with a term GPA of **3.75** or higher, with no incomplete or failing grades.

## 5. Course Repetition & Grade Forgiveness

- A student may repeat an undergraduate course in which a grade below **C** was received.
- **Grade Forgiveness:** For up to two repeated courses across a student's degree program, the higher grade earned replaces the lower grade in the calculation of the cumulative GPA, although both attempts remain listed on the official transcript.
- Subsequent course repeats average both grades into the cumulative GPA.
