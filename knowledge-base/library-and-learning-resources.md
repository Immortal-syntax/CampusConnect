# Library & Digital Learning Resources

> **Scope:** Institutional Libraries, Digital Catalogs, Research Databases, and Tutoring Centers  
> **Target Audience:** All Students, Researchers, Faculty

## 1. Library Facilities & Operating Hours

### 1.1 Facility Schedule
- **Main Campus Library:**
  - Monday – Thursday: 07:30 to 23:00
  - Friday: 07:30 to 19:00
  - Saturday: 10:00 to 18:00
  - Sunday: 12:00 to 21:00
- **24/7 Quiet Study Commons:**
  - Open continuously throughout the academic term.
  - Requires active Student ID card tap for late-night entry (22:00 to 07:00).
- **Extended Finals Week Schedule:** The main library operates 24 hours daily during the two weeks prior to and during final examinations.

### 1.2 Study Spaces & Collaborative Technology
- **Individual Quiet Carrels:** Available on Floors 3 and 4 on a first-come, first-served basis. Cell phone usage and conversational interaction are prohibited in quiet zones.
- **Collaborative Group Study Rooms:**
  - Reservable online via the library portal up to 7 days in advance.
  - Accommodates 3 to 10 students.
  - Maximum booking limit: 3 hours per study group per day.
  - Equipped with 4K interactive presentation screens, HDMI/AirPlay hubs, and writable dry-erase wall surfaces.

## 2. Circulation, Borrowing & Loan Privileges

### 2.1 Loan Allowances & Renewal Periods
- **Undergraduate Borrowing Limit:** Up to 25 physical books simultaneously.
- **Undergraduate Loan Duration:** 28 days for standard catalog items, with up to 3 automatic renewals unless an active hold/recall is placed by another student.
- **Graduate Borrowing Limit:** Up to 50 items for 90 days.
- **Media & Technology Equipment:** Laptops (MacBook / Dell XPS), scientific graphing calculators, mirrorless cameras, and audio microphones are loanable for 72 hours from the Circulation Desk.
- **Course Reserves:** Textbooks and core reading packets set aside by course instructors are available at the service desk for 2-hour in-library checkout.

### 2.2 Fines & Lost Item Replacement
- Standard collection items do not incur daily overdue fines, but accounts with items overdue by more than 14 days will be temporarily restricted until items are returned.
- Reserve materials and loanable tech carry an overdue charge of $1.00 per hour up to a maximum replacement charge.

## 3. Digital Research Databases & Interlibrary Loan (ILL)

### 3.1 Electronic Databases & Remote Access
Enrolled students have unlimited access to over 250 academic databases using institutional single-sign-on (SSO) credentials:
- **STEM & Engineering:** IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, ScienceDirect (Elsevier), SpringerLink, PubMed.
- **Social Sciences & Humanities:** JSTOR, Project MUSE, ProQuest Central, HeinOnline.
- **Business & Economics:** Bloomberg Terminal, Harvard Business Publishing, Wharton Research Data Services (WRDS).

### 3.2 Interlibrary Loan (ILL) Services
- When a required journal article or monograph is not owned by the university, students may submit a complimentary ILL request through the library dashboard.
- Digital articles are delivered directly via secure PDF within 24 to 48 hours. Physical loan items typically arrive within 4 to 7 business days.

## 4. Academic Tutoring & Writing Assistance

### 4.1 Academic Success & Peer Tutoring Center
- **Walk-in & Scheduled Tutoring:** Available for all lower-division mathematics, computer science, physics, chemistry, and accounting courses.
- **Coding Help Desk:** Peer coding mentors provide 1-on-1 debugging, algorithm review, and environment troubleshooting for foundational computer science courses.
- **Cost:** 100% complimentary to all enrolled undergraduate students.

### 4.2 University Writing & Communication Center
- Professional and peer writing consultants assist at any stage of the composition process: brainstorm outline, thesis statement formulation, citation compliance (APA, MLA, Chicago, IEEE), and graduate school personal statements.
- Consultations are offered both in-person and asynchronously via written draft review.
- The Writing Center does not provide proofreading as a standalone service; emphasis is placed on developing student self-editing skills.
