## 1. Student Health, Wellness & Counseling

### How can a student schedule a counseling or mental wellness appointment?
Students can access confidential counseling services via the Student Wellness Center. Consultations are available both in-person and virtually. In case of immediate crisis, 24/7 on-call counseling is accessible through the campus emergency desk.

### Are physical health consultations covered by student fees?
Basic consultations, minor injury treatment, and annual preventive screenings at the Student Health Clinic are covered under standard student health fees. Laboratory diagnostics or external specialist referrals may require primary insurance.

## 2. Library & Research Assistance

### What are standard library operating hours and study room bookings?
- **Main Library Hours:** Monday through Thursday 07:30 to 23:00; Friday 07:30 to 19:00; Saturday & Sunday 10:00 to 20:00.
- **24/7 Quiet Study Commons:** Accessible with a verified student ID badge.
- **Group Study Rooms:** Reservable online up to 7 days in advance for increments of up to 3 hours per study group.

### How do students access peer tutoring or academic writing workshops?
The Academic Success & Writing Center provides complimentary peer tutoring for core STEM courses, foreign languages, and writing projects. Appointments can be scheduled through the learning portal.

## 3. Financial Aid & Scholarship Inquiries

### When is the priority deadline for institutional scholarship applications?
The priority application window for institutional merit and need-based scholarships opens on October 1st and closes on March 1st for the upcoming academic year.

### How do emergency financial relief grants work?
Enrolled students facing unexpected emergencies (medical expense, temporary housing crisis) can apply for emergency relief grants through the Office of Student Affairs.
