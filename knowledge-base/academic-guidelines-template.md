## 1. Credit Hour & Course Registration Policies

### Minimum and Maximum Credit Loads
- **Full-time undergraduate status:** 12 to 18 credit hours per semester.
- **Credit overload policy:** Students with a cumulative GPA of 3.50 or above may petition for up to 21 credit hours with dean approval.
- **Part-time status:** Fewer than 12 credit hours per semester.

### Add / Drop Period
- Students may add or drop courses without academic penalty during the first 10 instructional days of each semester.
- Dropping a course after the designated census date results in a grade of "W" (Withdrawn) on the transcript, which does not impact GPA calculation.

---

## 2. Academic Advising & Prerequisite Checks

### Mandatory Advising
- All first-year and second-year students must schedule an advising session with their assigned academic counselor prior to course selection.
- Junior and senior students in good academic standing may register online through the institutional portal.

### Prerequisite Waivers
- If a student possesses equivalent prior learning or transfer credits, a prerequisite waiver form signed by the department chair must be submitted to the registrar.

---

## 3. Academic Standing & Honors Criteria

### Good Standing
- A cumulative grade point average (CGPA) of 2.00 or higher on a 4.00 scale.

### Academic Probation
- Students whose semester GPA drops below 2.00 are placed on academic probation for one semester and must complete an academic recovery plan.

### Dean's Honor List
- Awarded each semester to undergraduate students completing at least 12 graded credit hours with a term GPA of 3.75 or above.
