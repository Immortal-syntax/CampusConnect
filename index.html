Meet the CampusConnect AI Assistant

The CampusConnect AI assistant is being engineered with Botpress Cloud to function as a knowledgeable, supportive collegiate guide. It is designed to understand complex student questions and pull verified information from university regulations.

What the Assistant Can Answer:

  • ✓ Academic Policies: Add/drop deadlines, degree requirements, prerequisite waivers.
  • ✓ Exam Schedules & Rules: GPA grading scales, conflict makeup procedures, honor rolls.
  • ✓ Learning Resources: Library commons hours, database access, free peer tutoring.
  • ✓ Campus Support: Mental wellness appointments, residential life, emergency relief.

Social Impact & Purpose

Bridging Information Gaps in Higher Education

Higher education administrative systems often present steep navigational hurdles for learners. First-generation students, commuter learners, and working students are disproportionately affected by fragmented offices and delayed responses during advising periods.

CampusConnect AI directly addresses this challenge by providing an accessible, 24/7 student guidance hub. By uniting a clean, responsive web interface with a real Generative AI assistant powered by Botpress Cloud, we democratize access to academic information, study planning, and wellness resources.
